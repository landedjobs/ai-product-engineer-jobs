<summary><b>How this list is built</b></summary>

This list is generated from landed's live job database and refreshed every two days. It keeps open roles posted in the last 90 days that link to an employer application. US-based and worldwide-remote roles come first, then the rest of the world. Within each group, roles from top-rated companies (by TrueUp lists, Y Combinator, valuation, funding stage and investors) come first among roles posted in the last 30 days, then newer roles. Each company appears at most 5 times, and staffing agencies are left out. Company badges come from TrueUp and Y Combinator data.
